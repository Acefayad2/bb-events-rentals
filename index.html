About Us

Your Event, Beautifully Handled

B & B Events and Rentals is a DMV-based husband-and-wife team dedicated to making every celebration feel effortless. Whether it's an intimate gathering or a full-scale affair, we handle the planning, decorating, coordinating, tents, and rentals — so you can simply enjoy the moment.
